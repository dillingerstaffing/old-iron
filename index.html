03 / FAQ

Does a school with about 40 retired desktops qualify for free pickup?

If the desktops are reusable, yes. We provide free pickup, insured logistics, and per-serial NIST 800-88 wipe certificates. Any non-reusable material is identified first, with recycling fees quoted up front.

How do I get proof my drives were wiped?

Every drive wiped under NIST 800-88 gets a per-serial certificate listing the method used. You receive the full set for your lot.

What happens after I email about a pickup?

Send a list of what you have. Get back a pickup window and an up-front quote for any recycling fee. Once the drives are processed, receive per-serial NIST 800-88 wipe certificates.

Do you take equipment that no longer works?

Yes. Working gear is refurbished for reuse. Dead equipment is recycled responsibly. Non-reusable material carries a recycling fee, quoted up front before pickup.
