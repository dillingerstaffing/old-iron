03 / FAQ

Does a school with about 40 retired desktops qualify for free pickup?

If the desktops are reusable, yes. We provide free pickup, documented custody, and per-serial NIST 800-88 wipe certificates. Any non-reusable material is identified first, with recycling fees quoted up front.

How do I get proof my drives were wiped?

Every drive wiped under NIST 800-88 gets a per-serial certificate listing the method used. You receive the full set for your lot.

What happens after I email about a pickup?

Send photos of what you have (a list works too). We reply within 24 hours with a pickup window and an up-front quote for any recycling fee. Once the drives are processed, receive per-serial NIST 800-88 wipe certificates.

Do you take equipment that no longer works?

Yes. Working gear is refurbished for reuse. Dead equipment is recycled. Non-reusable material carries a recycling fee, quoted up front before pickup.

Can we mail in devices from departing employees for certified wipes?

Yes. Mail or drop off the drives or laptops from departing staff; we wipe each to NIST 800-88, issue a per-serial certificate for every drive, and send a monthly batch report for your records. Flat per-asset fee, quoted before you ship. Ask about mail-in wipe: Open in GmailEmail app

Selling your laptop or desktop on Marketplace?

Drop it off or mail it in before you list it. We wipe the drive to NIST 800-88 and hand you a per-serial wipe certificate to put in your listing photos, so your buyer knows none of your data goes with the machine. Flat per-device fee, quoted before you ship or drop off. Ask about a seller wipe: Open in GmailEmail app
